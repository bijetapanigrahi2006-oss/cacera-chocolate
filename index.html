II · The Origin

From the heart of the cacao tree

Every CACERA bar begins as a single pod, hand-cut from a tree that has been tended by the same families for generations.

We work with fewer than a dozen smallholder farms in the Sambirano Valley, where volcanic soil and river mist give the beans their bright red-fruit character. We buy only what those farms can grow well, and we pay far above the market to keep it that way.

III · Our Signature

What makes CACERA special

Three quiet promises, kept in every bar we make. Nothing is rushed, nothing is hidden, and nothing is added that does not belong.

I

Handcrafted in Small Batches

No more than two hundred bars leave our atelier in a single batch. Each one is stone-ground, tempered on marble and wrapped by hand, then numbered, so you always know exactly which batch you are tasting.

  • Stone-ground for 72 hours
  • Tempered by hand on marble
  • Hand-numbered, hand-wrapped in gold foil

II

Ethically Sourced Cocoa

We trade directly with the families who grow our cacao. There are no brokers and no guesswork. Farmers are paid well above Fairtrade minimums, and every bar can be traced back to the farm where its beans were grown.

  • Direct trade, long-term partnerships
  • Premiums reinvested in farming communities
  • 100% traceable from farm to bar

IV · The Craft

From bean to bar, by hand

Five patient steps separate a cacao pod from a finished CACERA bar. We take our time over every one of them.

  1. 01

    Harvest

    Pods are cut by hand at peak ripeness, only when their colour turns from green to deep gold and crimson.

  2. 02

    Ferment & Dry

    Six days in banana-leaf-lined boxes wake the flavour inside each bean. Then they dry slowly beneath the equatorial sun.

  3. 03

    Slow Roast

    Small, low-temperature roasts, profiled for each harvest, draw out caramel and toasted-nut depth without bitterness.

  4. 04

    Stone-Ground Conche

    Seventy-two hours between granite stones refine the chocolate to an impossibly silken texture.

  5. 05

    Temper & Wrap

    Tempered on marble for a mirror shine and a clean snap, then wrapped in gold foil and numbered by hand.
